To vote for the Spirit of Unity Award on the website, follow these steps:

  1. Log in as a player.
  2. Navigate to the "Squads" page.
  3. Select any team by clicking "Show Squad."
  4. Once the list of players appears, click on a player's photo. The photo will flip, revealing the back.
  5. On the back of the photo, you'll see a handshake icon. Click on it to cast your vote.
  6. After voting, the player's and team's handshake count will increase.
  7. Individual players vote summary will be available in user dashboard
