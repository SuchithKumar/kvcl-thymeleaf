Rules and Regulations

General Information

  1. Tournament Dates :  The KVCL-2024 Cricket Tournament will take place on September 27th, 28th, and 29th, 2024.

  2. Eligibility : All players must belong to the Arya Vysya Community. Any deviation from this rule will result in the entire team being suspended from the tournament for three years. Additionally, this will tarnish the reputation of the regional Vasavi Sanga.

  3. Participation : The semi-finalists of the KVCL 9 Tournament will be divided into 4 groups.

  4. Team Submission : The names of all eleven players must be filled out, duly signed, and submitted to the organizers 30 minutes before the start of their respective match.

  5. Dress Code : Players must adhere to the dress code provided by the tournament organizers. Shorts and slippers are not allowed. Any deviation from this rule will result in a penalty.

  6. Cricket Ball : All matches will be played with Wilson 1 tennis ball.

  7. Cricket Bats : Teams are responsible for bringing their own cricket bats. Except Srilankan Bats and Fiber bats all wooden bats are allowed.

  8. Liability : The organizers are not responsible for any injury or accident that occurs to a player during the entire tournament.

  9. Attendance : Teams must be present for all days of the tournament, from the inauguration to the valedictory ceremony. Participation medals/certificates will be issued to all teams only on the last day of the tournament.

  10. Match Schedule : There will be no changes or shifts in the match schedule or plan at any given time. All players are requested to adhere to the schedule.

  11. Team Captain : The captain of the team cannot be changed at any point during the tournament unless he is injured and unable to play.

  12. Venue : The tournament matches will be played at National College Grounds, Shankarapura, Basavangudi, and APS College Grounds, NR Colony, Basavangudi. Spare Ground : National College Grounds, Jayanagar.

Tournament Rules

  1. Disciplinary Rules : 

    • Umpire Decision is the final decision and if any player/players get involved in over Argument, Abusive language used, Deliberate Physical contact, Verbal aggression will result in either of the below depending on the situation:

      1. First Warning or 5 Runs Penalty or Eliminated out of the Tournament
      2. If any 3 players from a single team are removed for the above reason, the entire team will be eliminated from the tournament
      3. Additionally, the specific player(s) will be blacklisted from current and future KVCL tournaments. This guideline will be strictly enforced.

    • Only one player can be enrolled/play for a team. If a player is seen playing for more than one team, then as a disciplinary action, whole team will be suspended from the tournament

  2. Key Rules : 

    • In KVCL – 2024, 20 Teams will be participating in this League come Knockout tournament

    • All the League matches and quarterfinals will be played for 8 Overs per innings, Whereas Semifinals & Finals will be played for 10 Overs per innings. Each participating team will mandatorily play 3 league matches in this tournament

  3. Groups & Points System : 

    • League Stage : 20 teams will be divided into 5 groups of 4 teams each and Every team will play 3 league matches against teams within the same Group. The Fixtures will be shared during the Inauguration. After every league match, the winning team is awarded 2 points and Top 2 teams from each group will enter Knockouts. If there is a tie in the number of points at the end of League stage, Tournament Net Run rate will be considered for getting the Top 2 teams from each group.

    • Knockouts : The top 2 teams from the 10 qualified teams, based on the most wins and highest Net Run Rate (NRR), will directly advance to the Semi-Finals. The remaining 8 teams will compete in the Quarter-Finals and Pre-Semi Finals for a chance to qualify for the Semi-Finals.

  4. Power play Rule : 

    • For 8 Over Matches: : During the first 2 overs, maximum of 3 fielders are allowed outside 30 yards’ circle. For the next 6 overs, a maximum of 5 fielders are allowed outside the 30 yards' circle.

    • For 10 Over Matches: : During the first 3 overs, maximum of 3 fielders are allowed outside 30 yards’ circle. For the next 7 overs, a maximum of 5 fielders are allowed outside 30 yards’ circle.

    • At any instance, the captain cannot keep more than five fielders (Excluding bowler) on leg side.

  5. Time Rule : 

    • 8 Over innings should be completed within 35 minutes from the start time of the play.

    • 10 Over innings should be completed within 43 minutes from the start time of the play. There will be a Penalty of 5 Runs per over if the above rule is not followed (UmpireDecision)

  6. Only 1 bowler can bowl a maximum of 3 overs in 8 overs & and 2 bowlers can bowl a maximum of 3 overs in 10 overs match.

  7. Free Hit is applicable for all kinds of No Balls & field replacements can be changed only if the batsman has crossed over.

  8. Only 1 bouncer (below head height) allowed in an over. Subsequent bouncer will be declared as a no ball in the same over.

  9. Authority for allowing a runner or a substitute fielder solely rests with the umpire depending on the injury suffered during the match.

  10. If the match is tied, it shall be decided upon Super over Basis.

  11. If there is a tie in the super over, the winner is typically decided by the highest number of boundaries (6’s & 4’s) scored throughout the match.

  12. If the ball is obstructed by a tree or any structure inside the ground, it is considered a ball in play.

  13. Throw/ Action bowling can be appealed by the batsman, but the umpire will decide further.

  14. There is no Mankad rule in the Tournament.

  15. All other International Cricket basic rules must be followed

Note : In the event of any discrepancies, the organizers will have the final say.
